At 1366x768 and at 1280x720, in both themes, the first screen of `/codespec/` has the eyebrow, the
headline, part of the 80-word lede and the code pair. "Get started" and the other three actions start
at 905 px, below the fold. The page is 7.7 screens long. About 4 of those screens are the
"0.1.2. Here is exactly what exists." section, which repeats all 29 rows of the Status page.

Effect: a first-time reader sees no next step without scrolling. The navbar's "Get started" link is the
only one in view, which is why the screen-only user still takes 1 action for `task-install`. The rest of
the page makes the reader scroll through the status list again.

Fix direction (one line): shorten the lede and move the code pair so the actions sit in the first
screen, and replace the status list with the KPI row and a link to Status (product.json; the hero
layout itself is in `@beyond10x/docs-system`).

Severity S2: it's the first screen of the site, at both gating viewports, and it's the biggest single
cut available on the landing page.

Fixed in R-03: the hero copy aligns to the top of the hero, the lede is 3 sentences, the status section and its KPI row are gone (Status is one action away); main on the home page holds at most 700 words.
