On every docs page (18), at 1366x768 and 1280x720, in both themes: scrolled to the end, the sidebar
`nav[aria-label="Docs sidebar"]` moves up with its container as the footer comes in; on
`/codespec/docs/reference/cli` at 1366x768 its top is at y = -467 of a 694 px box. At the top of the
page it is fully in view. This is Docusaurus's stock behaviour (the sticky sidebar ends with the doc
container).

Fix direction (one line): keep the sidebar's sticky box in view down to the footer, or accept the
footer state in the adapter by a design decision.

Severity S3: only at the page end, where the navbar stays in view and the footer repeats the links.
