The report gives the scroll containers at the gating viewports as one number against the last
round's. In R-07 it rose from 18 to 24. The report named no page, and the result files of R-06 were
gone, so the cause (wider tables on three pages after UXF-024) took a forced-style Playwright run
to find.

Effect: a layout side effect of a change set is visible only as a total; the next triage cannot
see where it lands without a probe.

Fix direction (one line): list the scroll containers per page with the widest of each, against
the last round's list, in `round.json` and `report.md`.

Severity S3: costs the operator a probe per round; no reader sees it.

Fixed in R-07: `ux/core/round.ts` `scrollContainerPages` and `scrollContainerLines` write
`scroll_container_pages` to `round.json` and a "Scroll containers at the gating viewports" section
to `report.md`. R-08 is the first round with a last-round column.
