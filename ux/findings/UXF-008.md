The static-analysis feature (`codespec effects`, `reach`, `trace`, the syscall barrier) takes 6,745
words over four pages, 23 % of the site. The pages sit in Concepts, Guides and Examples. The same
netcheck worked example appears three times: as a table in the concept page, run step by step in the
guide, and generated in full on the netcheck page. `codespec trace` is explained in the concept page and
again on Call trees. Searching "trace" lists five pages. The top hit is "Traces" on the harness page,
which uses the same word for seeded inputs.

Effect: a reader who wants the syscall report doesn't know which of the four pages to read, and reads
the same example two or three times.

Fix direction (one line): give each page one job: the concept explains (no worked example), the guide
runs it once, and netcheck plus Call trees become one generated example.

Severity S2: it's the largest overlap on the site and directly against the round's goal to reduce
content. Generated pages: the change to netcheck and Call trees lands in
`crates/docs/src/netcheck.rs` and `crates/docs/src/calltrace.rs`.
