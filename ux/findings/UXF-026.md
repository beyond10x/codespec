Triage rule 4 asks whether each change of the last set moved its target. Most targets are
regression assets in `ux/tests/l2-findings.spec.ts` and `crates/docs`, and the round runs none of
them. In R-05 and R-06 the targets were checked by hand, outside the round.

Effect: the round's report shows a change set's targets only when they are a round metric (in
R-06, one of five).

Fix direction (one line): run the L1 specs and `l2-findings.spec.ts` in round mode and list each
result in `report.md` and `round.json`.

Severity S3: costs the operator a manual check each round; no reader sees it.

Fixed in R-06: `ux/core/specs.ts` gives the spec pattern of each mode; a round runs the L1 specs and `l2-findings.spec.ts` too, and `ux/core/round.ts` writes their results to `round.json` (`regression_assets`) and a "Regression assets" section of `report.md`. The Rust assets stay under `cargo test -p codespec-docs`.
