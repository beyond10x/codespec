Release 0.12.0 added the Elixir, Gleam and Odin adapters, so the support matrix and its legend
hold nine languages, 18 role columns (12 before). Each role column was at least 3.4rem wide with
0.5rem padding on each side, 54 to 57 px, so the matrix needed 1239 px and the legend 1276 px in
a 946 px article column at 1280x720. The UXF-021 and UXF-034 regression test failed at both gating
viewports. Gate 1 stayed green because the tables scroll inside their own box.

Effect: the matrix scrolls sideways again at every desktop width, the defect UXF-021 closed.

Fixed in the merge: in `website/src/components/SupportMatrix/styles.module.css`, role headers,
cells and counts have 0.2rem side padding and no minimum width, and the role headers are set in
lower case with normal letter spacing. Language headers have 0.3rem side padding. In the legend,
a state's meaning wraps (11 to 13rem) instead of widening the first column. At 1280x720 the
matrix and the legend fill 946 of 946 px. The narrowest width they take is 894 px for the matrix
and 889 px for the legend. A tenth language, two more columns of about 36 px, would no longer fit
at 1280x720, and this regression test would fail again.

Severity S3: nothing is hidden, but the widget scrolls sideways.
