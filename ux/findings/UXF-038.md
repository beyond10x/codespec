Release 0.12.0 added the Elixir, Gleam and Odin adapters, so the support matrix has 18 role columns
of 128 feature rows, about 2,300 cells, up from about 1,540. The L2 contract runs axe on every page
in every theme at both gating viewports. On this page axe takes 36 to 43 s alone. With two workers
on a loaded host, the page's units reach the 60 s test bound. The whole Playwright step of ux-check
takes 5 to 8 minutes with 2 workers, well over the test budget (a step within 120 s, no test over 60 s). The R-08 round had the same timeouts (T16, T17).

Effect: the every-merge gate is red, or close to red, on a loaded machine for a reason unrelated to
the UI. Its time keeps a build slot for minutes.

Fix direction (one line): bring the step under 120 s and every unit under 60 s. For example: run
axe once per page and theme instead of once per viewport, or run axe on the matrix once outside
the per-page units. Raising the timeout is not a fix.

Severity S3: the UI is not affected; the gate's budget is.
