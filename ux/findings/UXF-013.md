The generated pages are 15,600 of the site's 28,700 words. They show every document in full and open:

- Greeter prints the `cir/1` JSON of `Count`, an 8-line function, twice: read (160 lines) and passed
  (120 lines).
- netcheck has a 158-line block.
- The cir/1 reference is 34 field tables in a row, 30 screens long.

Series already folds its long files into `<details>`. Greeter and netcheck don't.

Effect: the reader scrolls past hundreds of lines of JSON to reach the next step of the walkthrough.

Fix direction (one line): fold every block over about 40 lines into `<details>` (as series does), show
the passed `Count` as a diff against the read one, and split or fold the cir/1 reference per node
family. Generators: `crates/docs/src/showcase.rs`, `netcheck.rs`, `cir.rs`.

Severity S3: the content is correct and the TOC helps, but this is where most of the content reduction
is.
