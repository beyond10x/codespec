`task ux-round` counts the result files that exist. A unit that fails before its end (a timeout)
writes no file, so the report prints "checked 93" where 96 were expected and says nothing of the
three that are missing. The round never runs the ratchet, so a red ratchet shows only as a number
in a table, and the Playwright totals (7 failed of 362) are not in the report.

Effect: a reader of `report.md` takes a partial round for a full one and misses that the gate it
feeds would fail.

Fix direction (one line): `writeRound` derives the expected units from the scenarios × viewports ×
themes, lists the missing ones, states the ratchet's verdict with its problems and the Playwright
totals.
