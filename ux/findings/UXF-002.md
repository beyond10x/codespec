axe-core reports `link-in-text-block` (serious, WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color) on 13 of the 19 pages, in
both themes and at both gating viewports: a link inside a paragraph has no underline and its colour
contrast with the surrounding text is below 3:1, so it is visible only as a colour change. All of
R-01's 180 serious nodes at the gating viewports are this rule but for UXF-004's two.

Pages: concepts/cir-1, concepts/static-analysis, examples/greeter, examples/series,
getting-started, guides/add-an-adapter, guides/map-a-repository-to-syscalls and others; the full
list is in `ux/rounds/R-01/round.json` (`measured`, key `axe.serious` per page scenario).

Fix direction (one line): underline links in running text (`.markdown p a`), in both themes.

Severity S2: a gate 5 failure on most pages; reading and following the links still works.
