UXF-024 brought markdown tables to 14 px and left the support matrix with its own sizes, pointing at
UXF-021; UXF-021 covers its sideways scroll and the cut descriptions, not its type sizes. In the
matrix the feature id, its description and the code in it are the smallest text on the page, the
code at 10.9 px. On Status the column and group headers are 10.9 px and the status chips 12 px.

Effect: on the two pages a reader uses to decide whether codespec handles a construct, the
construct's name is set below the 14 px floor. Contrast passes (axe 0 serious).

Fix direction (one line): set the matrix's feature cell and its code, and the Status group headers
and chips, to 0.875rem (the component styles, or an override in `website/src/css/custom.css`).

Severity S3: readable, below the floor heuristic 15 sets.

Fixed in R-07: the support matrix sets its feature ids, descriptions and their code at 0.875rem (`website/src/components/SupportMatrix/styles.module.css`); `website/src/css/custom.css` sets the Status table's group and row headers and its status chips at 0.875rem, headers in sentence case. The matrix keeps its width (848 px at 1366x768); its descriptions stay cut to one line (UXF-021).
