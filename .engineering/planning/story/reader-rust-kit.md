# Story: The Rust reader claims the remaining kit features

## Outcome

`codespec-read-rust` claims and passes the kit features it can read and does not claim today
(89 of 120 verified), each through a `conformance/cases/<case>/rust/case.rs` that
`codespec-run-rust` runs to the case's `expected.txt`. The 31 open ones: `decl.embedded`,
`decl.interface`, `decl.type_params`, `decl.union`, `decl.var`, `decl.variadic`,
`expr.call_value`, `expr.extern`, `expr.extern_method`, `expr.float`, `expr.func_ref`,
`expr.global`, `expr.iface_call`, `expr.instantiate`, `expr.lambda`, `expr.nil`,
`expr.spread`, `expr.to_iface`, `expr.zero`, `intrinsic.sorted_keys`, `stmt.foreign`,
`type.any`, `type.array`, `type.extern`, `type.float`, `type.func`, `type.param`,
`type.tuple`, `type.unit`, `unop.plus`, `unop.recv`.

## Acceptance

- The Rust `read` column has at least 105 of 120 `verified`; `reader-rust.json` has `fail` 0
  and `conforms: true`.
- Each of the 31 still unclaimed is named in the crate doc of `adapters/rust-read` with its
  reason: no Rust source form (`unop.plus`), not runnable at step 4 (the three extern features,
  `story:reader-check-externs`), or the reason found. At most 15 stay unclaimed. The crate doc today names `stmt.foreign`, `unop.plus`, `unop.recv`,
  `type.any` and `expr.nil`; `decl.variadic`, `expr.spread` and `decl.embedded` have no Rust form
  (`website/data/conformance/writer-rust.json`). A feature of these the unit finds a form for is
  claimed.
- `decl.union`: the case source is added here; `story:reader-check-evaluator-step` claims it
  later. `stmt.foreign` stays unclaimed.
- Every feature claimed before is still `pass`; `task conformance`, `task adapters-check` and
  `cargo test -p codespec-read-rust` exit 0, with a reader test per newly mapped construct.

## Scope

- Own: `adapters/rust-read`, `conformance/cases/*/rust/`; `adapters/rust-run` only where a new
  case source needs the runner to call it. Inferred.
- Shared with the unit in the other slot, resolved by the coordinator at merge: `CHANGELOG.md`
  (each adds its own entry), `AGENTS.md` (its own rows), `Taskfile.yml` (its own steps),
  `conformance/cases/<case>/` (a unit adds only files of its own language to an existing case and
  never edits its `case.cir.json`, `calls.json`, `expected.txt`, `features.json` or `case.go`).
- A unit may add a variant case directory (`crates/conformance/src/lib.rs` § `features.json`: a
  second case of a subject in a form the language can write), built with `task conformance`, and
  names each one in its report. Counts of cases are read on the merged head.
- Regenerated by the coordinator on the merged head, never committed by a unit:
  `conformance/reader/<lang>/**`, `website/data/conformance/*.json`,
  `website/data/support-matrix.json`, `website/data/status.json` (`task docs-generate`);
  `examples/*/*.cir.json`, `examples/*/out/**`, `examples/netcheck/*`,
  `website/docs/examples/*.md` (`task examples`, `task examples-netcheck`);
  `adapters/groovy/testdata/lending.read.cir.json` where a JVM change rewrites it.
- `cir/1` does not change. A feature that needs a change of `crates/model` is reported and left
  unclaimed.
- The measure is the count of features with state `verified` in the reader's `read` column of
  `website/data/support-matrix.json`, of the 120 a reader can claim: the 127 of `kit.json` less
  the seven `fact.*`, which `crates/model/src/manifest.rs` refuses in a reader manifest.
