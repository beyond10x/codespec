# Story: The Quarkus quickstart reads with at most ten refusals

## Outcome

The Java reader maps what it refuses today in the pinned `hibernate-orm-panache-quickstart`
(`adapters/java/README.md` § A quickstart: 25 refusals in 15 functions) apart from the 8 throws
of a jar exception: the persistence annotations `Entity`, `Cacheable`, `Column`, `Id`,
`GeneratedValue`, a second constructor, a nested class, a static field that is not a constant,
and a field of an entity or of `PanacheEntity`.

## Acceptance

- Reading the quickstart at its pinned commit against the class path of
  `adapters/java/testdata/quickstart` gives at most 10 refusals, at most 2 of them not a jar
  throw; `adapters/java/README.md` states the new count and each remaining refusal with its
  reason.
- The report states whether a throw of a jar exception can be read in `cir/1` as it stands the
  way a throw of the package's own checked exception is, with the evidence; it is not built here.
- The checked-in fixture `adapters/java/testdata/quarkus` gains one source per construct of the
  Outcome; `task java-quarkus-check` reads it twice, byte-equal, with no refusal for those
  constructs, against the regenerated `adapters/java/testdata/quarkus.read.cir.json`.
- `codespec effects` over `adapters/java/testdata/quarkus` still reports
  `FruitResource.create` as `db`; each persistence annotation is recorded in the module the way
  `transactional` is today, asserted by a reader test.
- Groovy guard: `reader-groovy.json` still has `conforms: true` and loses no `pass`;
  `adapters/groovy/testdata/lending.read.cir.json` changes only through its generator.
- `task adapters-check` and `task conformance` exit 0; the Java `read` column loses nothing.

## Scope

- Own: `adapters/java`, `adapters/jvm`, `website/docs/guides/read-a-quarkus-project.md`.
  Inferred. Runs after `story:reader-java-kit` in the same slot: both edit
  `adapters/java/src/codespec/read/Reader.java`.
- Shared with the unit in the other slot, resolved by the coordinator at merge: `CHANGELOG.md`
  (each adds its own entry), `AGENTS.md` (its own rows), `Taskfile.yml` (its own steps),
  `conformance/cases/<case>/` (a unit adds only files of its own language to an existing case and
  never edits its `case.cir.json`, `calls.json`, `expected.txt`, `features.json` or `case.go`).
- A unit may add a variant case directory (`crates/conformance/src/lib.rs` § `features.json`: a
  second case of a subject in a form the language can write), built with `task conformance`, and
  names each one in its report. Counts of cases are read on the merged head.
- Regenerated by the coordinator on the merged head, never committed by a unit:
  `conformance/reader/<lang>/**`, `website/data/conformance/*.json`,
  `website/data/support-matrix.json`, `website/data/status.json` (`task docs-generate`);
  `examples/*/*.cir.json`, `examples/*/out/**`, `examples/netcheck/*`,
  `website/docs/examples/*.md` (`task examples`, `task examples-netcheck`);
  `adapters/groovy/testdata/lending.read.cir.json` where a JVM change rewrites it.

  unclaimed.
