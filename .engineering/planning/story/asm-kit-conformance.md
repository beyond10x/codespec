# Story: The AArch64 and RISC-V 64 writers, the cross-check, and the site

## Outcome

`adapters/asm` gains `codespec-write-aarch64` and `codespec-write-riscv64`, the same program as
`codespec-write-x86-64` of `story:asm-x86-64-writer` over the other two backends. The gate runs
`codespec conform` for both as writers, each a part of `scripts/check-parts.txt`, and a
cross-check that the outputs of the three writers are byte-equal on every case all three claim.
The site's support matrix (`website/data/support-matrix.json`) and `website/data/status.json`,
both generated by `codespec-docs`, list the three writers with their claims. Both writers read the
claim list `adapters/asm/src/claim.rs`, so the features the lowering stories add there are
conformed on AArch64 and RISC-V 64 by these parts from the day this story lands.

## Done When

- A failing test first: `the_aarch64_writer_runs_op_add`, the kit case `op-add` through
  `codespec-write-aarch64` and `codespec-run-asm`, equal to `expected.txt`.
- `codespec conform --lang aarch64 --role writer` and `--lang riscv64` each report
  `conforms: true` with every case of `adapters/asm/src/claim.rs` passing (at least the 78 of
  group 1).
- The cross-check fails when one target's output for one case differs, shown by a test that
  feeds it a changed output.
- `codespec-docs generate --check` covers the three writers; `website/data/support-matrix.json`
  has a column for each of `x86-64`, `aarch64` and `riscv64` with its claims, and
  `website/data/status.json` a capability naming the three writers and the check that holds them.
- The two conform parts and the cross-check pass in CI (the emulators are installed by the
  backend stories).
- Each part under 120 s, stated in the pull request.

## Scope

- Own: `adapters/asm/src/bin/codespec-write-aarch64.rs`, `adapters/asm/src/bin/codespec-write-riscv64.rs`,
  `crates/cli` (the two `--lang` values), `scripts/check-parts.txt` (two conform parts and the
  cross-check), `Taskfile.yml`, `crates/docs` (the three writers in the matrix and the status),
  `website/data/*` (generated), `AGENTS.md` (the gate table), `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Reads `adapters/asm/src/claim.rs`; never writes it.
- After `story:asm-x86-64-writer` and `story:asm-riscv64-backend`.
