# Story: The reader check runs what the Go writer refuses through the evaluator

## Outcome

Step 4 of the reader check (`crates/conformance/src/conform.rs`: pass, write as Go, run) stops
with `refused` when `codespec-write-go` refuses the module. That caps every reader below the
features it reads: `decl.union`, which the Go writer refuses. (`stmt.foreign` of a non-Go reader
stays `refused`: the evaluator stops at a foreign statement, `docs/evaluator.md` E4.) When the
Go writer refuses, the check runs the passed module with the programs `codespec-write-cir` and
`codespec-run-cir` of `story:eval-conformance-runner`, looked up in `--adapters` like the Go pair.
`crates/conformance` gains no dependency on `crates/eval`, which depends on it.

## Acceptance

- A reader case whose passed module the Go writer refuses is judged by the evaluator's output:
  equal to `expected.txt` is a pass, different is `fail`, an evaluator stop is `refused` with
  the evaluator's reason. The report's feature entry says which runner judged it. A test in
  `crates/conformance` holds the three outcomes on fixtures.
- A case the Go writer writes is judged as before: no verdict of the five checked-in reader
  reports changes except from `refused` or `fail` to `pass`.
- `decl.union` is added to the claim of each of the Rust, Java and C readers whose corpus holds
  the case and whose case then passes; a reader whose case does not pass keeps it unclaimed with
  the reason in its documentation.
- `docs/conformance.md` § 6 and the module doc state the rule. The report format stays readable
  by `read_report`; a new field is optional and documented.
- `task check` steps `conformance` and `docs-check` exit 0.

## Scope

- Own: `crates/conformance`, `docs/conformance.md`, `crates/docs` only if the report field
  needs it, and the one claim line of each reader manifest. Inferred. Needs
  `story:eval-conformance-runner` merged; runs after the reader kit stories and not beside
  `story:reader-c-kit-conventions`, which edits `conform.rs` too.
- Shared with the unit in the other slot, resolved by the coordinator at merge: `CHANGELOG.md`
  (each adds its own entry), `AGENTS.md` (its own rows), `Taskfile.yml` (its own steps),
  `conformance/cases/<case>/` (a unit adds only files of its own language to an existing case and
  never edits its `case.cir.json`, `calls.json`, `expected.txt`, `features.json` or `case.go`).
- A unit may add a variant case directory (`crates/conformance/src/lib.rs` § `features.json`: a
  second case of a subject in a form the language can write), built with `task conformance`, and
  names each one in its report. Counts of cases are read on the merged head.
- Regenerated by the coordinator on the merged head, never committed by a unit:
  `conformance/reader/<lang>/**`, `website/data/conformance/*.json`,
  `website/data/support-matrix.json`, `website/data/status.json` (`task docs-generate`);
  `examples/*/*.cir.json`, `examples/*/out/**`, `examples/netcheck/*`,
  `website/docs/examples/*.md` (`task examples`, `task examples-netcheck`);
  `adapters/groovy/testdata/lending.read.cir.json` where a JVM change rewrites it.
- `cir/1` does not change. A feature that needs a change of `crates/model` is reported and left
  unclaimed.
- The measure is the count of features with state `verified` in the reader's `read` column of
  `website/data/support-matrix.json`, of the 120 a reader can claim: the 127 of `kit.json` less
  the seven `fact.*`, which `crates/model/src/manifest.rs` refuses in a reader manifest.
