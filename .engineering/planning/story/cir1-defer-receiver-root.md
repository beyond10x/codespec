# Story: `defer` as a statement and a receiver root on call refs

## Outcome

`cir/1` gains the two additive items of `docs/query-language.md` § 8: a `defer` statement
(`{k: "defer", call}`) read by the Go reader instead of `foreign`, and `recv_var` on
`CallRef.method` and `iface`, so a query can match an Unlock to the Lock's value.

## Acceptance

- The Go reader reads `defer mu.Unlock()` as a `defer` statement; the fixture that was `foreign`
  before has no refusal for it.
- The Go reader writes `recv_var` on method and interface call refs; a reader test asserts the
  field on a fixture with `c.mu.Lock()` and `defer c.mu.Unlock()` (method refs) and on a call
  through an interface value `w.Write(p)` (iface ref).
- `lock-without-unlock` written in cq gives the same findings as `codespec check` on its hit and
  miss fixtures (`crates/check/tests/fixtures/lock-without-unlock`), and its entry is removed from
  the list of waiting checks that `story:query-core` checked in.
- A conformance case for `defer` exists with its feature in `schemas/cir-1.features.json`; the Go
  writer writes it and `codespec conform --lang go --role writer` still exits 0; a writer that
  cannot express it refuses it by name; the evaluator runs it and
  `codespec conform --lang cir --role writer` still reports `conforms: true`.
- `schemas/cir-1.schema.json` is regenerated in the same commit; documents without the new fields
  read unchanged (additive), asserted by the existing example checks.
- `task check` exits 0 on the wave branch.

## Out of Scope

- `panic` and `recover`, and the `string`/`[]byte` conversion (`docs/evaluator.md` § 9).
